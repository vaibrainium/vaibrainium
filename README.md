### PhD Candidate in Computational Neuroscience · University of Washington, Seattle

I build probabilistic models and ML systems for understanding how the brain makes decisions. My work sits at the intersection of experimental neuroscience, Bayesian inference, and production software engineering.

Currently finishing my dissertation on decision-making, with methods spanning drift diffusion modeling, dimensionality reduction, and large-scale neural data analysis. On the engineering side, I built **NeuRPi** — a distributed, real-time closed-loop experiment control system used in active neuroscience research.

I'm looking for **ML Scientist** and **Research Scientist** roles in AI, health tech, and neurotech/BCI.

- 🌍 Based in Seattle, WA
- ✉️ [vaibhavt459@gmail.com](mailto:vaibhavt459@gmail.com)
- 🔬 Dissertation: probabilistic modeling of decision-making under uncertainty
- 🛠️ Building: [NeuRPi](https://github.com/vaibrainium/NeuRPi) — distributed system for closed-loop neuroscience experiments
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on ML, neuroscience, and BCI projects
